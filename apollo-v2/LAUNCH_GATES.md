# Launch gates | Explicitly not authorized

A pull request, CI success, audit or testnet experiment is **not** permission to launch or sell tokens.

1. **Identity and ownership:** establish rights to LeadCoin/LDC names, domain and historical contracts; document whether this is a new token, a legal successor, or an unrelated prototype.
2. **Business purpose:** approve specific non-investment use cases and user protections; do not connect to managed trading payouts, profit shares, or investment returns without licensed legal structuring.
3. **Tokenomics:** authorize supply, vesting, ownership distribution, controlled treasury custody and public disclosures. Deploy with a vetted multisig, not a personal hot wallet.
4. **Security:** all compiler/test/coverage/fuzz/invariant checks green, independently audited, and review issues resolved.
5. **Compliance:** obtain competent legal opinions for sale/distribution, securities and commodities law, money services, AML/sanctions, tax and consumer protection in intended jurisdictions.
6. **Operations:** scoped testnet rehearsal, reproducible verified sources, monitoring, incident response, address verification and emergency plan.
7. **Authority:** owner explicitly approves a separate transaction-by-transaction deployment proposal and final contract parameters.

No live deployments, token sales, client-account integrations or treasury transfers are in this PR.
