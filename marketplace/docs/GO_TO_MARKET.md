# Apollo Leads | First customers, not first token

## Wedge market
Miami-Dade and Broward businesses buying qualified appointment opportunities. Start with ONE vertical, e.g. home-service quotes, rather than all industries at once. The dashboard illustrates three verticals for validation.

## Proposed customer discovery (not completed)
- Interview 10 verified buyer businesses; document what counts as a chargeable lead and what invalidates a lead.
- Recruit 2-3 willing pilot buyers in a single vertical with explicit consent and lead-source contracts.
- Measure source acquisition cost, verified contact rate, booked meetings, qualified conversions, disputes, churn and actual net margin.
- Test subscription fee against pay-per-accepted-lead with explicit dispute/refund terms.
- Do not distribute third-party personal data or make automated calls/texts without appropriate permissions.

## Product moat hypothesis
Better provenance and outcome verification, transparent lead scoring, partner capacity awareness, freshness and feedback loops. Future AI can be trained on authorized outcomes; the current scoring function is explainable heuristics, not AI.

## Blockchain decision
Do not launch token or promise utility/value. Only revisit if a concrete use case is demonstrated that materially improves trust/settlement compared to ordinary ledgers and meets legal/licensing obligations.

## Evidence standards
Separate *demo*, *pilot*, *contracted*, *invoiced*, *collected* and *retained-revenue* metrics. Never label mocked payouts as actual revenue.
