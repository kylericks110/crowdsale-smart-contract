# Production gates | Not approved

1. **Founding buyer evidence:** recruit verified businesses and validate service-area fit, lead acceptance requirements, willingness to pay and cost to acquire legitimate leads.
2. **Identity and authorization:** real authentication, SSO/MFA, least privilege, tenant-level authorization and isolation, audit trail, encryption and key rotation.
3. **Consumer provenance:** compliant opt-in flows with signed evidence, exact seller disclosures, explicit recipient/channel scope, timestamp, source URL, consent language/version, revocation and deletion workflows. Contractual provenance verification for third-party lead sources.
4. **Contact safety:** Do Not Call/suppression lists and legal controls for calls, texts, email, location and sector-specific rules, including Florida and federal requirements. No outbound messaging until reviewed and approved.
5. **Operations:** durable database, reliability/replay/idempotency, anti-fraud, duplicates, lead recency, capacity locking, transaction reconciliation, quality disputes, monitoring and incident response.
6. **Business:** pricing experiments with real contracts and billing, partner payouts only with documented policies, clear refunds, tax and privacy documentation.
7. **AI:** train only on legitimate permitted, de-identified data; backtest outcome calibration and bias; human review for high-stakes routing; never fabricate conversion or profit forecasts.
8. **Security:** dependency SCA, DAST, penetration test and explicit production launch review.
9. **Legal:** trademark/IP review regarding historical LeadCoin; contract and advertising compliance. Token launch or rewards plans require a separate regulatory analysis.
10. **Launch authority:** a separate owner-approved scoped rollout and security signoff. This PR grants no deployment authority.

All current leads and partners are invented for demos. There are no real buyers, sales, commissions or revenue represented.
